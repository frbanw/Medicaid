The Challenge

"Long-term care can cost more than most families have saved."

Medicaid can help cover nursing home and home care costs, but the eligibility rules, look-back review, and paperwork are complex, and mistakes can be costly.

You need counsel who knows the rules and will guide your family through them.

What We Do

Medicaid Legal Services

Practical guidance for seniors and families planning for long-term care.

01

Medicaid Planning

Plan ahead to protect savings, a home, and other assets while positioning a loved one to qualify for Medicaid-covered long-term care.

02

Nursing Home Medicaid

Guidance through eligibility, the look-back review, and the application process when a loved one needs nursing home care.

03

Home Care (Community) Medicaid

Help qualifying for Medicaid-covered home care so a loved one can continue living at home.

04

Medicaid Applications

Preparation and filing of Medicaid applications, including gathering financial records and responding to agency requests.

05

Trusts & Asset Protection

Medicaid asset protection trusts and related planning tools tailored to your family's goals.

06

Denials, Appeals & Fair Hearings

Representation when an application is denied, benefits are reduced, or a fair hearing is needed.
